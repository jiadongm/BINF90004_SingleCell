The data

Single-nucleus RNA-seq of human heart across three developmental stages — foetal, young and adult — from nine donors, three per stage.

Sim CB, Phipson B, Ziemann M, et al. Sex-Specific Control of Human Heart Maturation by the Progesterone Receptor. Circulation. 2021;143(10):1614–1628. doi:10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.120.051921

Practicals adapted from Advanced Methods for Single-Cell RNA-seq Analysis by the Phipson Lab, WEHI — quality control, integration, annotation and differential expression modules by Belinda Phipson and Melody Jin.
